# Verified sources and reading tasks

1. Sakar, C. and Kastro, Y. (2018). Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention Dataset.
   UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI: 10.24432/C5F88Q. CC BY 4.0.
   https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/468/online+shoppers+purchasing+intention+dataset
   Use for provenance, attributes, target, and limitations. It does not support causal sales uplift.

2. Sakar, C. O., Polat, S. O., Katircioglu, M., and Kastro, Y. (2019).
   Real-time prediction of online shoppers' purchasing intention using multilayer perceptron and
   LSTM recurrent neural networks. Neural Computing and Applications 31, 6893-6908.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s00521-018-3523-0
   Institutional publication page:
   https://research.bau.edu.tr/en/publications/real-time-prediction-of-online-shoppers-purchasing-intention-usin-2/
   Original work distinguishes aggregated features from sequential clickstream. Public CSV is not
   an equivalent source of sequences for an LSTM. Abstract/bibliographic record checked, not full paper.

3. Real-Time Prediction of Online Shoppers' Purchasing Intention Using Random Forest (2020).
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7256375/
   Prior research already studies session/user information available upon connection. Read the full
   paper and export its exact author/venue citation; do not claim arrival-context prediction is novel.

4. scikit-learn: Probability calibration.
   https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html
   Supports reliability diagrams and distinction between ranking and probability quality.

5. scikit-learn: Common pitfalls and recommended practices.
   https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html
   Supports training-only preprocessing and data-leakage precautions.
